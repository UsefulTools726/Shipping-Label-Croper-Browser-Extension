Privacy Policy — Shipping Label Cropper

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Shipping Label Cropper (“the Extension”) processes shipping label PDFs that you open in your browser so they can be cropped for printing.

Data we collect
The Extension does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information to the developer or any third-party server.

Local processing
PDF files are fetched and processed on your device in the browser. Cropped results are opened as local PDF tabs (blob URLs).

Local storage
The Extension may store on your device only:

learned crop templates (fingerprint + crop coordinates)
related extension settings
This data stays in the browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and is not uploaded by the Extension.

Permissions
Host and tab-related permissions are used only to read the active tab’s PDF URL, fetch that PDF for cropping, and provide the toolbar / right-click actions. The Extension does not track your browsing history.
